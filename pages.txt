Redesign the entire authentication experience of the Quarterly Reports Management System using a clean enterprise SaaS design inspired by modern platforms like Atlassian, Jira, Confluence, Microsoft 365, and other professional internal business tools.

LAYOUT

Use a fully centered authentication layout.

Structure:

1. Company Logo (centered)
2. Authentication Title
3. Subtitle
4. Form Fields
5. Remember Me / Forgot Password section
6. Primary Action Button
7. Social Login Buttons (optional)
8. Sign In / Sign Up switch links
9. Footer Section

The authentication card should be vertically centered on desktop and comfortably positioned on mobile.

Do NOT use split-screen layouts.

Do NOT place the logo in the top-left corner.

Move all branding into the authentication card.

LOGO

Place the Quarterly Reports logo centered at the top of the card.

Use the custom "R" logo mark and wordmark.

Title:
"Log in to continue"

"Access your reports, forms and workflows securely"

Use color sparingly.

90% of the interface should be white, slate gray, and light gray.

- Primary actions
- Active navigation
- Focus states
- Links
- Important highlights

The final design should feel like a premium enterprise platform used by organizations to manage secure reports, forms, approvals, file uploads, and role-based workflows.
